Twelve points enumerate what a defensible AI audit trail must capture. Seventeen points describe what a verifiable AI audit trail must do. PRD-3 is a specification — five thousand seven hundred forty-three lines of normative text, fifty test-vector corpora, an exit-code contract, a reference verifier shipped as a separate static binary the regulator runs locally. The reader who has reached this paragraph has the inventory the next examination cycle will eventually ask for. The chain proves what the AI said and that the record was not tampered with after capture; it does not prove the AI's statement was correct. That distinction is in the spec too — §1.2, epistemic scope — because the brand promise here is honest about what the chain does and what stays outside it.
