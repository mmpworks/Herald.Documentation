A defensible AI audit trail in 2026 captures, at minimum, the twelve fields the cross-regulator literature now agrees on. The list is real. It is also a list of *requirements* — what the regulator wants to see — rather than a list of *mechanisms* — how the system actually produces what the regulator wants to see. This page walks the twelve points in the order the literature presents them, in Herald's voice, and shows the section of the PRD-3 chain-of-custody specification that proves each one. Then it walks five more — mechanisms the twelve don't reach that the specification does. Twelve plus five is seventeen. The reader who has read both lists side by side has the comparison the regulator will eventually make.
