## 🔐 9:00 AM — Day 1 — §10.68 operational dual-algorithm seal under §4.3.2

Raj took the projector. He brought up the seal-attestation architecture.

The chain-of-custody substrate had migrated from CNSA 1.0 single-algorithm seal to CNSA 1.0 + CNSA 2.0 hybrid dual-algorithm seal seventy-two weeks ago. The migration ran in three operational stages: parallel-seal stage (six weeks; both attestations computed and recorded; only CNSA 1.0 attestation validated for chain integrity), agreement-enforcement stage (eight weeks; both attestations computed and validated, with hybrid agreement attestation MAC-bound at composition), and operational stage (fifty-eight weeks ongoing; both attestations validated, hybrid agreement attestation MAC-bound, refusal-at-capture on any seam where either algorithm's attestation fails or where hybrid agreement is invalid).

Each chain row in the operational stage carried three cryptographic attestations under §4.3.2:

- `classical_seal_attestation_sha256` — SHA-3-512 hash of the CNSA 1.0 attestation (RSA-4096 or ECDSA-P521 signature) over the chain row payload
- `pqc_seal_attestation_sha256` — SHA-3-512 hash of the CNSA 2.0 attestation (ML-DSA-87 signature; ML-KEM-1024 used for key encapsulation; SLH-DSA-256s used for the HSM-attestation-of-modules chain) over the chain row payload
- `hybrid_seal_agreement_attestation_sha256` — SHA-3-512 hash of the composed agreement attestation (the two algorithm outputs concatenated and signed under both algorithms, with a structured agreement-attestation document carrying both algorithm-output references)

The verifier in the operational stage validated all three attestations independently and validated the agreement attestation against the two component attestations. A clean run produced verdict `exit_code: 0` plus six additional verifications on the verdict object: `classical_seal_attestation_verified`, `pqc_seal_attestation_verified`, `hybrid_seal_agreement_verified`, `algorithm_suite_manifest_matches_active_certification`, `pqc_migration_stage_consistent`, and `dual_algorithm_attestation_freshness_within_policy`.

Raj brought up a sample seven-day verification run from us-west-pacific-3 Oregon. 1.4 billion chain entries across the seven days. Zero refusal-at-capture events. Zero agreement-attestation invalidations. Average verifier latency: 8.3 milliseconds per chain row in dual-algorithm mode (compared to 2.1 milliseconds per chain row in CNSA 1.0 single-algorithm mode — the ML-DSA-87 verification is the dominant cost).

Dawn watched the verifier output stream past on the projector. The verdict line for a representative row read:

```
exit_code: 0

pqc_seal_attestation_verified: true
hybrid_seal_agreement_verified: true
algorithm_suite_manifest_matches_active_certification: true
pqc_migration_stage_consistent: true
dual_algorithm_attestation_freshness_within_policy: true
```
