## Framework-side issue categories

Three categories of framework-side issue have surfaced. Definitions hold across chapters.

| Category | Definition | First chapter | Canonical instance | Recovery path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Speculation** | Auditor invents anchors to fill silences | Ch01 | Compositional security; the catwalk demo; coverage-boundary primitive | Cover-memo prose; institution culture |
| **Under-reporting** | Framework misses findings the reference spec catches | Ch04 | Atrio §10.15 invariant 5 + §10.18 missing cross-reference | Cover-memo cross-walk; institution catches internally |
| **Inarticulability** | Finding exists; reference spec articulates it; no Kognitos field can file it under any reading | Ch05 | Helmstad April 15 patient (§1.2 (c) chain-clean / source-wrong) | Borrow reference-spec language verbatim; explicitly note no field fails |

**Severity ordering.** Inarticulability is structurally worst because no engagement-team rigor can recover the missing finding. Under-reporting is structurally worse than speculation because findings vanish from the audit deliverable rather than just from the working notes. Speculation is recoverable through extra labor at culturally mature institutions.

## Stakes-pattern observations

How the three framework-side issue categories behave as engagement stakes scale up.

| Stakes level | Speculation behavior | Under-reporting behavior | Inarticulability behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Routine compliance** | Auditor's extra labor; recoverable via cover memo | Findings can vanish; institution catches via culture | Rare; less load-bearing when it occurs |
| **Regulator inspection** | Speculation produces variance between audit teams | Findings vanish from the deliverable that goes to the regulator | Cover-memo borrowing required (Helmstad pattern) |
| **Litigation defense / public-safety** | Speculation cannot anchor evidentiary claims | Findings vanish at exactly the stakes where they should be most visible | **Framework cannot supply the litigation file** (Pacific Crescent pattern) |

**Key signal.** Stakes do not change what the framework can articulate. Under Kognitos, a routine compliance audit and a public-safety litigation-defense audit produce the same framework-shaped deliverable: twelve rows, no epistemic-scope clause, no §1.2 distinctions, no §1.1 Daubert mapping. Institutional stakes are not a degradation lever the framework can pull.

The natural defense of any minimal framework — *"it's a baseline; institutions add what they need"* — does not hold for inarticulabilities. The institution cannot add a row to Kognitos that captures the §1.2 (c) epistemic-scope line; that line is a meta-property of the audit-trail's evidentiary claim and the framework's row-shape does not admit it. The recovery path is to use a different framework.
